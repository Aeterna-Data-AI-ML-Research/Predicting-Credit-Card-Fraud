The pooled comparison then counts cross-fold pairs between a fold-normalized candidate and a saved champion on its original scale. Use within-fold AUC deltas as the primary estimand. A row bootstrap of fixed predictions measures sampling uncertainty conditional on those scores. It misses shared training, recipe selection, and dependence from duplicate or related rows.

- **Pooling can manufacture apparent rank improvement.** Round 6 rank-transforms scores separately within each fold. The pooled comparison then counts cross-fold pairs between a fold-normalized candidate and a saved champion on its original scale. Use within-fold AUC deltas as the primary estimand. A row bootstrap of fixed predictions measures sampling uncertainty conditional on those scores. It misses shared training, recipe selection, and dependence from duplicate or related rows.
